About Him

He Began his professional voice-over career at just eight years old, initially getting involved through his father's work on shows like Power Rangers.

Notable roles:

  • Eren Yeager in Attack on Titan
  • Kirito / Kazuto Kirigaya in Sword Art Online
  • Inosuke Hashibira in Demon Slayer
  • Rin Okumura in Blue Exorcist
  • Meliodas and Zeldris in The Seven Deadly Sins
  • Makoto Naegi in Danganronpa
  • Cat Noir in Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug & Cat Noir
  • Shirou Emiya in Fate/stay night: Unlimited Blade Works
  • Caesar Anthonio Zeppeli in JoJo's Bizarre Adventure
  • Silver the Hedgehog in the Sonic the Hedgehog series
